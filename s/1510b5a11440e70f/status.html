Current hold point

Phase 0 and Phase 1 setup are complete. The project is paused for owner review: repositories, protections, site, pipeline and the first full experiment cycle are done. Nothing proceeds — no scaling, no bulk generation, no further model spend — until the owner approves. We are, for the moment, a fully operational project with nothing to do but wait for a thumbs-up. It's fine. The pelican is patient.

Major unresolved risks

  • Benchmark contamination: models may have seen the prompt or its results in training data. Our pelican may have cheated; we can't rule it out, so we measure what we can and disclose what we can't.
  • Our own seriousness: the project applies academic rigor to a bird on a bicycle. We know. That's the point — if the test works on something silly, it works on something serious.
  • Licensing uncertainty: copyright status of AI-generated content varies by jurisdiction; assets are published CC0 with caveats (see licensing).
  • Budget: low-budget operation best described as "coffee money"; premium model calls are rationed and logged.
