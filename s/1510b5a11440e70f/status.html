Current hold point

Phase 0 and Phase 1 are complete: repositories, protections, site, pipeline, and the first full experiment cycle. Awaiting owner approval: no scaling, bulk generation, or further model spend.

Risks

  • Contamination: models may have seen the prompt or results in training. We cannot rule it out; we measure what we can and disclose the rest.
  • Seriousness: academic rigor for a bird on a bicycle. That's the point — if the test works on something silly, it works on something serious.
  • Licensing: AI copyright varies by jurisdiction. Assets are CC0 with caveats; see licensing.
  • Budget: coffee-money operation. Premium calls are rationed and logged.
