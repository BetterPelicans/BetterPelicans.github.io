Current hold point

Phase 0 and Phase 1 setup are complete. The project is paused for owner review: repositories, protections, site, pipeline and the first full experiment cycle are done. Nothing proceeds — no scaling, no bulk generation, no further model spend — until the owner approves.

Major unresolved risks

  • Benchmark contamination: models may have seen the prompt or its results in training data; effects are measured and disclosed, not assumed away.
  • Image generation: the image-generation path (ChatGPT OAuth) is not available in the current session; visual references may need to be produced via CLI tooling or procedural construction.
  • Licensing uncertainty: copyright status of AI-generated content varies by jurisdiction; assets are published CC0 with caveats (see licensing).
  • Budget: low-budget operation; premium model calls are rationed and logged.
