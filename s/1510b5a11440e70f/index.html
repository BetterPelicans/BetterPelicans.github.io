The test

Since October 2024, our informal text-to-SVG exam has been:

Generate an SVG of a pelican riding a bicycle.

Simon Willison originated the prompt in October 2024, explained its difficulty, and published the matching benchmark repository. He inspired this project, but he did not create or run this site and is not affiliated with it. We took the idea extremely seriously.

It tests distinct objects, plausible riding, depth, and a valid, standalone, editable SVG. See docs/benchmark.md for canonical wording, variants, and sources.

Why this test

A convincing result needs a recognizable pelican, a rideable bicycle, and unmistakable riding. That tests composition, physics, depth, and vector craft in one tiny file.

Because the prompt has been public since 2024, models may have seen it or its results in training. That makes benchmark contamination part of the test. Strong performance from exposed models matters; we measure what we can and state what remains unknown.

What counts as good

  • A proper pelican — pouch, beak, dignity — on a bicycle that could roll.
  • Honest depth: bird on saddle, feet on pedals.
  • Newer models outperforming older ones.
  • Repairs saving near-misses, with failures recorded.
  • If a contamination signal exists, report it plainly.

How we test

One fixed prompt; documented settings; 8–12 independent candidates; deterministic validation; specialist critique; at most three repair rounds; then a publication checklist. We hash and log every artifact. Full procedure: How we test.
